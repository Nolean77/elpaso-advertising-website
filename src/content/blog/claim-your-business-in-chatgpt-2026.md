On November 10, 2026, two days before this post, OpenAI opened a beta called Businesses in ChatGPT. A business can now claim an official profile inside ChatGPT, prove it is the real owner, and manage the details it provides. This post is a checklist, not a forecast, built on OpenAI's own announcement and Help Center article.

If you want the wider picture of how AI tools describe local businesses, we covered that in [our April post on AI search](/blog/AI-Search-Is-Quietly-Changing-Where-El-Paso-Customers-Find-Your-Business-in-2026/). This one stays on the new claim-and-verify process.

## What OpenAI just opened in ChatGPT

OpenAI's [announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) describes two pieces. The first is Business Hub, where a business can claim its profile, manage its information, and see insights on how people engage with it on ChatGPT. Once verified, a company can update key details and add self-service product feeds with pricing and availability. The second piece is Business Agents, covered further down.

OpenAI pitches the launch as a way to reach "the 1.2 billion people who use ChatGPT each week." That number is OpenAI's own claim from the announcement. It is not an independent count, and it says nothing about El Paso or Juárez.

For insights, OpenAI lists the number of mentions and website clicks, plus discovery topics, which it describes as common themes across conversations where your website is cited. Insights are aggregated and only show up once there are enough conversations or interactions to meet certain thresholds. And OpenAI says it does not share users' conversations or Business Agent chats with businesses. You see patterns, not people.

## Who can claim right now, and who cannot

According to the [Help Center article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt), you can claim a business if you own it or are authorized to represent it. Eligible businesses can claim an existing listing or add one where ChatGPT is available and the required verification is supported, and availability depends on the business's location and type. You do not need an Ads account or a ChatGPT Enterprise agreement to claim.

One limit matters for storefronts. The same article says local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing U.S. listing. Business Agents are narrower still. They are for eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the U.S. that have a website and meet OpenAI's Business Agents Policy.

The pages give no country-by-country list and do not promise that every verification will succeed. OpenAI plans to expand to more markets and business categories over time, with no dates attached. If your business is in Juárez or elsewhere in Mexico, open Business Hub and see what it offers rather than assume either way.

## How claiming and verification work

The Help Center lays out the claim path in four steps.

1. Sign in to ChatGPT and open Business Hub from the sidebar.
2. Search for your business by name and select the matching listing.
3. Confirm the ChatGPT account you want to use.
4. Choose a verification method and follow the prompts.

Methods include a business phone (SMS or automated call), a business email, your website domain (a DNS TXT record), and Shopify. Shopify merchants can connect their store from Business Hub or the Shopify admin to streamline verification. Shopify is one method among several, and it is not required to set up an agent.

Then comes a separate second step. Once your claim is approved you can access Business Hub, but identity verification with Persona is required before you can publish changes, connect a product feed, view insights, or create an eligible agent. If you verified the claim another way, you can finish Persona later. Persona may ask for a government-issued ID, business registration information, and proof of authorization, and review time varies. Submitting is not the same as approval. If you use Persona to create a brand-new profile, approval publishes it automatically, so continue only when your details are ready.

Some housekeeping from the same article. If someone else already claimed your listing, you can dispute ownership from the profile's more-options menu. A claim invitation is a personal email link, so do not forward it to give someone access. The admin who claimed the business can add teammates or grant an agency access.

## What a claimed profile lets you control, and what it does not

The Help Center FAQ is direct. Claiming lets you manage the information you provide, and it "doesn't guarantee increased visibility or give you direct control over ChatGPT's independent recommendations." OpenAI adds that advertising does not influence ChatGPT's independent answers.

Claiming alone does not add a verified badge, since the blue check comes after Persona approval. A product feed does not publish an agent or guarantee that ChatGPT recommends your products. ChatGPT may also use other sources when answering questions about your business, and updates can take time to appear.

Our reading is simple. Treat a claimed profile as an accuracy tool, not a ranking lever. Because ChatGPT can still draw on other sources, we suggest keeping your website and Google Business Profile accurate in parallel. That advice is ours, not OpenAI's, and nothing in these pages says a ChatGPT profile replaces either one.

## Business Agents, the U.S. ecommerce option

A Business Agent is a chat that answers from the business's own information. OpenAI says that conversation is clearly labeled, separate from the customer's original ChatGPT conversation, and distinct from ChatGPT's independent answers. Businesses can sponsor their agent and use it as the destination for ChatGPT Ads, and sponsored placements are labeled and kept separate from ChatGPT responses.

Per the FAQ, agents cannot complete purchases, bookings, or order lookups. They can explain products and policies and link to your website. Customers can only chat with a published agent, and a draft agent created through Shopify still has to be reviewed and published. You cannot create an agent while verification is pending or after it fails. Some businesses can manage a profile without being agent-eligible.

## What El Paso, borderplex, and Mexico businesses should do this week

This section is our reasoning and practical next steps. The examples are hypothetical.

- **A U.S. shop or restaurant in El Paso.** Search Business Hub for your existing listing and claim it if it is eligible. Plan for Persona before you expect to edit anything or see insights.
- **An online seller, including one in Juárez who ships into the U.S.** Open Business Hub and see which verification methods it offers you. If domain or Shopify verification appears, it may be the natural fit.
- **A local service business, like a plumber or a salon.** You may be able to manage a profile, but agents have separate eligibility requirements.
- **Everyone.** Do not expect an immediate change in traffic. Check insights once they appear, and remember that a blank metric may mean the reporting threshold has not been met yet, not that nothing happened.
- **Holiday season.** We suggest keeping hours and offers current on your website and Google Business Profile at the same time, since ChatGPT may use other sources too.
